    What this map can—and can’t—say

    The public sources describe device parts, mineral markets, and farm labor. Sayari trade records name who shipped and received Vuse products, but customs paperwork does not prove which factory made them, and Sayari risk flags are screening leads, not findings. Industry risk is not proof about an individual product.

    How to read the trail

    • “Unknown” means this project has not established the link.
    • A country-level risk is a research lead, not a product finding.
    • Open the sources to check what each one actually supports.
