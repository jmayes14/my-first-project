# Plan

## Goal
Create a small, research-first visual map that makes vape supply-chain stages,
material origins, ethical-sourcing evidence, and unknowns easier to understand.

The first release is a representative supply-chain map, not a map of every
brand or a verified account of a specific product. Keep it feasible within a
few hours a week and avoid conclusions beyond the available evidence.

## First release
1. Show a representative path from raw materials through component
   production, assembly, and distribution in `docs/index.html`.
2. Let visitors filter the map by topic and select a stage to inspect its
   evidence, sources, and unknowns.
3. Add sourced origins, organizations, and ethical-sourcing information as
   research becomes available.
4. Label what is supported, uncertain, or not yet researched; make source
   limitations visible.
5. Check keyboard access and readability on desktop and mobile, then publish
   the map with GitHub Pages.

## Current starting point
- The map includes sourced, industry-level context on device components,
  cobalt mining, and tobacco-farming labor concerns.
- Stage details and topic filters are interactive in the browser without a
  framework or build step.
- No evidence yet connects those materials or risks to a particular vape
  product, supplier, or factory.
- The exact evidence standard, product focus, and success measure can be
  refined as research progresses.
- No editor, accounts, live data, or comprehensive supplier database are
  required for this first release.

## Deferred
- A detailed evidence and confidence rubric
- A product- or brand-specific trace
- Structured data, editing tools, and permissions
- Automated collection, live supplier monitoring, or broad industry coverage

## Release check
- Every factual sourcing or labor-risk claim has a source and is phrased within
  what that source supports.
- Unresearched and unverified information is visibly distinguished from
  confirmed information.
- The map remains readable on a phone and clearly explains its limits.
