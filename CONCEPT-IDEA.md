# Concept Idea

## Problem
I want to understand whether there is child labor or other human-rights concerns in the vape supply chain, and I want to understand the ethical sourcing of vapes.

## Who has this problem
The people most directly affected, based on what I said, are young teen users. I also want to understand the issue for people who care about where vape materials come from and whether the supply chain is ethical. (inferred)

## Why this seems real
The concern is tied to materials and supply chain steps such as lithium-ion mining and tobacco farming, and the idea is to trace those links in the vape industry. This is based on what I said during the interview, not on a research review.

## What people do today instead
At this stage, I do not have a clear existing alternative or process identified. That means the current approach may be limited to informal checking, scattered information, or no clear traceability process at all. (inferred)

## What success would look like
The first deliverable is a visual map of a representative vape supply chain, covering material and component origins as well as ethical-sourcing evidence. For now, success means making the stages to investigate and the gaps in available information easy to see. A more specific evidence standard and success metric can be defined as the research develops.

## Time and budget
This is a small project with a limited time commitment: a few hours a week. I do not have a defined budget yet. (inferred)

## What I am deliberately not building
This project is not meant to be a full compliance platform, a legal determination, or a global supply-chain database. It is also not meant to cover every issue in the wider consumer product industry. (inferred)

## Who this is for
This is for people who want a clearer view of vape supply-chain traceability and ethical sourcing, especially in a format that can be researched and visualized. The main audience can be refined later.

## Open questions
- What counts as acceptable evidence for child labor or human-rights risks in a vape supply chain?
- What does ethical sourcing mean in this project, and what is in scope versus out of scope?
- Which specific materials, organizations, or products can be traced with public sources?

These details can be refined after the initial map is in place. Until then, the map should show research gaps explicitly and avoid unsupported claims.

## Summary
This project is a focused attempt to trace the vape supply chain and understand whether ethical sourcing and human-rights concerns are visible in that chain. The first deliverable is a simple visual map of representative stages, with sources and unknowns made visible as research is added. The plan is to keep it research-based and narrow enough to fit a few hours a week.
